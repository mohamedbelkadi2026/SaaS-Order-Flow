Find where isActive or enabled flag is toggled for Google Sheets integration.

2. When reactivated (isActive = true/1): resume syncing from where it left off
3. The sync job must CHECK isActive before processing each Google Sheet integration

Search for the Google Sheets polling/sync logic (likely in a setInterval or cron job) and add:
